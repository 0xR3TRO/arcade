## Music

The "Classic" music style in R3TRO Blocks plays *Korobeiniki*, a traditional Russian folk song from the 19th century that is in the public domain. The arrangement is synthesized in code.

## Sounds and graphics

All graphics are drawn in code (Canvas, CSS or inline SVG) and all sounds are synthesized live with the Web Audio API. No images, sprites or audio samples from other games are used.
